N

Nexus Billing Portal

A billing platform for Nexus Billing Group, who handle billing and insurance verification for chains of hospitals and clinics — replacing their reliance on third-party billing software with an in-house system to cut long-run costs.

What I did: built the frontend from scratch — RBAC-gated views, credentialing workflows, denials management, payment pipelines, notifications, and the dashboard and dark sidebar navigation. M

MetriCorr

Frontend developer on MetriCorr at Codility Solutions — a monitoring platform for industrial clients whose factory pipelines are fitted with sensors, with readings visualized on dashboards via charts. Since sensors could misfire or return imprecise readings, we built a manual portal mirroring the automated workflows: operators could enter or correct readings by hand, fill in the same operational forms, and track performance through the same reporting charts as a reliable fallback.

Impact: led a 3-person team to deliver in 1 month against a 2.5-month deadline (all pre-AI-assisted tooling), then spent the remaining 2 weeks on thorough testing — a 30-day time saving that helped win the client into a 3-year long-term engagement.

  • React
  • Data Visualization
  • Codility Solutions
T

Taxology

Frontend developer on Taxology at Codility Solutions — cloned an existing WordPress site and rebuilt it as a React application, matching the original design and content on a modern component-based frontend.

Impact: the client was hitting recurring plugin and platform limitations on WordPress; moving to a React frontend removed those constraints and gave them a more scalable foundation to build on going forward.

  • React
  • WordPress → React

E

ERP Pack

Frontend developer on ERP Pack at Codility Solutions — a large-scale ERP application with modules for employee data, attendance, payroll, accounts, HR, and admin. Built around two core modules: an Employee module, and an Admin module split into Super Admin (the product owner, with control across all client organizations) and Admin (scoped to a single client organization).

Impact: built as a multi-tenant product, not just an internal tool — architected to onboard and serve up to 100 client organizations, each isolated under its own Admin.

  • React
  • RBAC
  • Codility Solutions
W

Workhub

An all-in-one work hub built at Coeus Solutions — the idea was a single product bundling the tools an organization would otherwise piece together from separate apps, the way Google bundles Meet, Calendar, Sheets, and Tasks.

What I did: built the frontend across three modules — an Admin module for role-based product control; a Scheduling module for meetings, tasks, agendas, and attendee/representative assignment on a calendar view; and a Monitoring & Calls module giving employers a live dashboard of employee online/offline status (with periodic webcam snapshots during work hours) plus built-in video calling with in-call chat and call-quality ratings.
